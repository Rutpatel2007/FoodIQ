1.   Flexible and reliable source of secondary income

Our partners can generate earnings all 365 days of the year
with full flexibility to login at any time of the day or day of
the week. However, most of them prefer to work with us
part-time to generate a secondary source of income for
themselves. A lot of delivery partners work with Zomato
seasonally, some work for specific parts of the day and so
on.

As you can see in the chart on the right, over the years,
there has been a consistent decrease in average days
logged-in per year and average hours logged-in per day

2. ​ Steady increase in earnings

The average monthly earnings for our delivery partners
(calculated for delivery partners who logged-in for at least
eight hours per day and 26 days per month) has increased
to ~INR 28,000 in CY24. Even after accounting for
estimated fuel cost (~INR 5,000 per month), it is fairly
attractive vis-à-vis alternate income opportunities for
them.

3. ​ Financial protection in the hour of need

All our delivery partners are by default covered under our
delivery partner insurance policy for accident, death and
health cover, amongst other benefits. As you can see, the
number of claims processed in favour of our delivery
partners has more than doubled over the last four years to
INR 53 crore per annum. 55% of the claims in 2024 were
paid to cover for medical expenses unrelated to accidents.

Given the scale of our network today, we feel grateful for the opportunity to positively impact so many
livelihoods and are more conscious than ever of the responsibility that we carry towards their welfare.
