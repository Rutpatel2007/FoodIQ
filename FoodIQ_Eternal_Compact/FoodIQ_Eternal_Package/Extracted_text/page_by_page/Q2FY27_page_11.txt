Q12. What is new and exciting at Hyperpure?
Akshant: Hyperpure now serves about 85,000 restaurants, up from 62,000 a year ago. We see three things
driving that growth.

   1.​ Speed. We now deliver within two hours in all major cities, and 25% of our orders already arrive
       within that window, and growing. This changes how restaurants procure. With a dependable
       two-hour option, they no longer need to plan and store large quantities of perishables, and we think
       that will steadily shift more of their purchasing to us.
   2.​ Formalisation. Most restaurants still buy through mandis and wholesalers, often in cash. That is
       changing as food safety and other compliance requirements tighten and operators look for
       consistent quality. Every Hyperpure order is fully invoiced and traceable, which makes us a natural
       choice as restaurants move to organised supply.
   3.​ Going beyond ingredients. We are becoming a broader supply chain partner for food businesses.
       We provide 3PL services to some of India's largest pan-India restaurant brands, and we now help
       restaurants set up complete kitchens, including equipment we source directly, some of it
       imported. Once a restaurant sets up its kitchen with us, we often go on to supply it with ingredients
       as well.

We compete with incumbent wholesalers and other B2B platforms that often compete on price alone. We
don't try to be the cheapest. We compete on reliability, speed and quality, which is what restaurants value
once they have tried us.
