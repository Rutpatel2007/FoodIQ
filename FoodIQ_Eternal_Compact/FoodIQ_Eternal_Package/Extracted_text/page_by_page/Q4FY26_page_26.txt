      Emphasis of Matter

      We draw attention to Note 6 to the consolidated financial results relating to the show cause notices
      (SCNs) and orders received by the Company from GST authorities in respect of GST on delivery
      charges. The Company, supported by the external expert's advice, is of the view that, it has a strong
      case on merits. Given the uncertainty involved, the ultimate outcome will be ascertained on the
      disposal of the above matter.

      Our opinion and conclusion is not modified in respect of this matter.
