Q4. Food delivery NOV growth continues to accelerate for the fifth straight
quarter (from 13.1% YoY in Q1FY26 to 21.5% in Q2FY27). Do we expect this trend
to continue?
Deepinder: We're happy with how the business is doing right now. That said, we try not to read too much
into any single quarter. Our long-term expectation for food delivery has always been 20%+ annual growth,
and the last two quarters have brought us back to that range. This view didn't change last year when
growth slowed, and it doesn't change now that it has picked up.

Over a longer (4-year) period, food delivery NOV has more than doubled, from INR 18,160 crore in FY22 to
INR 37,993 crore in FY26. That is a CAGR of about 20%, despite a slower FY26. In hindsight, part of last
year's slowdown came from some ordering occasions moving to quick commerce as it scaled. That shift
seems to have settled now.

Most of the recent growth comes from more people ordering, and ordering more often. Average monthly
transacting customers grew 16% YoY to 28.0 million, and a growing share of new users are ordering food
online for the first time, many of them outside our top eight cities.

There is still a lot of headroom. Restaurant food is a small part of how India eats. As cities grow, incomes
rise and more young people live away from home, we think the food delivery market can keep compounding
at 20%+ for many years.

In the near term, it is hard to say how any particular quarter will turn out. But the things we track closely,
such as app opens, new user acquisition and our share of the market, are all moving in the right direction.

Where we see good returns, we'll keep reinvesting in growth.
