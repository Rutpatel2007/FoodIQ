# FoodIQ Eternal Compact Research Package

This archive retains every non-PDF research file from the original Eternal package, including full-document extracted text, page-by-page text, Annexure OCR text, source register, and notes. It also contains all eight supplied Eternal PDFs in compressed form.

PDFs were recompressed with a screen-oriented profile where safe. The April 2025 company overview and Q4 FY26 shareholder letter were rasterized at 55 DPI to reduce unusually large image payloads. Their extracted text and page-indexed text are retained in the archive. Small-print visual verification may require the original uploaded PDF.

No non-PDF research files or source periods were intentionally omitted.
