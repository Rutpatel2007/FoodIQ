FoodIQ Institutional Research Methodology & Evidentiary Standards

The 6 Epistemic Evidence Tiers

  • REPORTED FACT: Directly extracted from audited corporate disclosure tables without modification.
  • DERIVED FACT: Mathematically derived via rigorous public accounting identities (e.g. Price x Volume).
  • MANAGEMENT EXPLANATION: Qualitative assertions and justifications made by executives in calls.
  • ANALYST INTERPRETATION: Deductions produced by research analysts with explicit assumptions.
  • HYPOTHESIS: Conjectures requiring empirical validation against newly released disclosures.
  • UNVERIFIED / ASYMMETRIC: Claims where disclosures are incomplete or company metrics diverge.

The 4 Comparability Governance Levels

  • LEVEL 1 (Directly Comparable): Identical accounting standards and units. Direct subtraction allowed.
  • LEVEL 2 (Comparable with Adjustment): Valid only after documented normalization (e.g. LFL District).
  • LEVEL 3 (Directionally Comparable): Trends benchmarked; absolute levels differ due to overhead allocation.
  • LEVEL 4 (Incomparable): Incompatible treatments (e.g. Blinkit 1P gross vs Swiggy 3P net). Direct cross-subtraction strictly blocked.
