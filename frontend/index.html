FoodIQ Institutional Research Methodology & Evidentiary Standards

The 6 Epistemic Evidence Tiers

  • REPORTED FACT: Direct number extracted from audited company tables without modification.
  • DERIVED FACT: Mathematically derived from reported facts via public accounting identities.
  • MANAGEMENT EXPLANATION: Qualitative justifications provided by management in letters or calls.
  • ANALYST INTERPRETATION: Analytical deductions produced by research analysts.
  • HYPOTHESIS: Plausible conjectures requiring data not currently disclosed in public filings.
  • UNVERIFIED / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: Claims where disclosures are incomplete or asymmetric.

The 4 Comparability Governance Levels

  • LEVEL 1 (Directly Comparable): Identical accounting standards and units. Direct subtraction allowed.
  • LEVEL 2 (Comparable with Adjustment): Valid only after documented normalization (e.g. LFL District).
  • LEVEL 3 (Directionally Comparable): Trends benchmarked; absolute levels differ due to overhead allocation.
  • LEVEL 4 (Incomparable): Incompatible treatments (e.g. Blinkit 1P gross vs Swiggy 3P net). Direct subtraction strictly blocked.
