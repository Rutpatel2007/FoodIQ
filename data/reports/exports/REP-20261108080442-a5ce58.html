Executive Summary

India's consumer-internet food and delivery ecosystem exhibits strong top-line scale alongside divergent operational economics. Eternal's Quick Commerce arm (Blinkit) has pulled decisively ahead in store network throughput, generating ₹849,924 / store-day (+22.0% growth since Q1 FY25) across 2,700 stores guided for FY27. In contrast, Swiggy Instamart has expanded AOV from ₹498 to ₹708 while contribution margins improved from -3.2% to -1.0%. Econometric testing reveals that synchronous quarter-on-quarter AOV jumps do not directly drive same-quarter margin turnaround (detrended r_delta = 0.1399, p = 0.72); rather, store vintage maturation and network density are the dominant drivers of economic break-even.
