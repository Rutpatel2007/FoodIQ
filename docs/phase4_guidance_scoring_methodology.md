# FoodIQ Phase 4: Guidance Scoring & Credibility Analytics Methodology

## 1. Mathematical Formulas & Delivery Metrics

FoodIQ rejects opaque black-box credibility ratings. All execution metrics are computed using explicit formulas disclosing numerators and denominators:

### 1.1 Strict Delivery Rate
Measures the proportion of completed commitments that were fully delivered without leniency:
$$\text{Strict Delivery Rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Delivered Count}}{\text{Evaluable Completed Count}} \times 100$$
Where:
$$\text{Evaluable Completed} = \text{Delivered} + \text{Partially Delivered} + \text{Missed}$$

### 1.2 Broad Delivery Rate
Credits partial deliveries with 50% achievement weight:
$$\text{Broad Delivery Rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Delivered} + 0.5 \times \text{Partially Delivered}}{\text{Evaluable Completed Count}} \times 100$$

### 1.3 Partial Delivery Rate & Miss Rate
$$\text{Partial Delivery Rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Partially Delivered Count}}{\text{Evaluable Completed Count}} \times 100$$
$$\text{Miss Rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Missed Count}}{\text{Evaluable Completed Count}} \times 100$$

## 3. Dissecting the Pending Commitment Asymmetry

Notice that Swiggy currently has 11 pending commitments vs Eternal's 2. 

### Why this is NOT an Execution Deficit
1. **IPO Timing:** Swiggy completed its public listing in November 2024 (late FY25). Its IPO Prospectus and subsequent Q1/Q2 FY26 shareholder letters introduced numerous multi-year strategic targets for FY27, FY28, and FY29.
2. **Horizon Maturation:** Labeling multi-year commitments (e.g. Out-of-Home ₹10,000 Cr by FY29) as misses would commit an egregious financial error.
3. **Execution on Matured Milestones:** On commitments whose deadlines *have* matured (e.g. FY26 Food GOV growth >20%, Q2FY27 store additions to 1,200+), Swiggy delivered 3 of 4 cleanly (75% strict, 87.5% broad).
4. **Eternal's Longer Public History:** Zomato / Eternal listed in July 2021, giving analysts a deeper mature dataset of resolved quarterly milestones (e.g. reaching 1,000 Blinkit stores in FY25, achieving 5.82% food delivery EBITDA margin).
