# FoodIQ Phase 3.1: Research Limitations & Reporting Asymmetries

## 1. Overview

While **FoodIQ** establishes rigorous comparability governance, institutional equity research requires explicit disclosure of all remaining reporting asymmetries, data gaps, and unresolved analytical boundaries.

The limitations cataloged below reflect structural constraints in public corporate disclosures by Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited, not deficiencies in data processing.

---

## 2. Catalog of Corporate Reporting Asymmetries

### 2.1 Undisclosed Dark Store Cohort Vintages & Active Store-Days
- **Asymmetry:** 
  - Swiggy computes dark store throughput and order density using internal **active operating store-days**.
  - Eternal discloses only **period-end closing dark stores** (e.g. 639 stores in Q1 FY25, 2,700 stores in Q2 FY27).
- **Analytical Impact:** 
  - Because Eternal opened hundreds of dark stores mid-quarter, effective active operating store-days were lower than $\text{Closing Stores} \times 91.25$. Consequently, actual operational throughput per active store-day for Blinkit was higher than the blended network estimate of ₹8.50 lakh / store-day.
- **Unresolved Data:** Month-by-month commissioning dates and vintage curves (e.g. throughput of stores $>12$ months vs $<3$ months) remain proprietary to management.

---

### 2.2 Asymmetric Order Volume Disclosures
- **Asymmetry:**
  - Swiggy regularly discloses quarterly order volume across segments (e.g. 130.3 mn Instamart orders in Q2 FY27).
  - Eternal discloses quarterly order volume for Food Delivery in certain glossaries, but **completely withholds order volume for Blinkit**.
- **Analytical Impact:**
  - Direct head-to-head benchmarking of orders per store-day (order density) between Blinkit and Instamart is impossible without external third-party traffic estimates.
- **Epistemic Classification:** Any peer claim regarding Blinkit order density is classified as **UNVERIFIED / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**.

---

### 2.3 Unbundled Promotional Subsidies & Loyalty Accounting
- **Asymmetry:**
  - Neither Eternal nor Swiggy publishes an audited reconciliation unbundling:
    1. Merchant-funded promotional discounts
    2. Platform-funded promotional vouchers
    3. Customer delivery fee subsidies
    4. Loyalty subscription accounting (Zomato Gold vs Swiggy One)
- **Analytical Impact:**
  - Raw take rates differ (Swiggy ~24.0% vs Eternal ~20.2%). While analysts deduce that underlying contractual restaurant commissions are within ~100 bps, this cannot be mathematically verified from public notes alone.
- **Governance Mandate:** Cross-company take rate comparisons must remain at **LEVEL 3 DIRECTIONALLY COMPARABLE**.

---

### 2.4 1P Inventory COGS & Product Gross Margin Opacity
- **Asymmetry:**
  - Following Blinkit's transition to a 1P inventory model in Q2 FY26, Eternal reports gross merchandise sales in Adjusted Revenue, but does not disclose category-level merchant wholesale acquisition costs (COGS) or inventory shrinkage.
- **Analytical Impact:**
  - External analysts cannot mathematically reconstruct what Blinkit's 3P agency commission revenue would have been post-Q1FY26.
- **Governance Mandate:** Peer comparison of Blinkit Adjusted Revenue against Swiggy marketplace Adjusted Revenue is classified as **LEVEL 4 INCOMPARABLE (STRICTLY BLOCKED)**.

---

### 2.5 Corporate Overhead & ESOP Allocation Discrepancies
- **Asymmetry:**
  - In Food Delivery and Quick Commerce segment reporting, Eternal excludes corporate management overhead and share-based payment (ESOP) expenses from segment Adjusted EBITDA.
  - Swiggy allocates certain centralized engineering, product development, and shared corporate overhead across its operating segments under different cost-apportionment keys.
- **Analytical Impact:**
  - The ~242 bps Food Delivery EBITDA margin spread in Q2 FY27 is economically indicative of relative profitability, but exact basis point spreads reflect differing corporate allocation conventions.

---

## 3. Forward Research Directives for Future Phases

1. **Synthetic Cohort Vintage Reconstruction:** If intra-year dark store capital expenditure additions are disclosed in annual cash flow statements, model estimated cohort age distributions.
2. **Subscription Economics Modeling:** Standardize membership program revenue recognition by tracking active Zomato Gold vs. Swiggy One subscriber disclosures.
3. **Regulatory Standardization Monitoring:** Track proposed MCA / SEBI disclosure guidelines for quick commerce inventory accounting to identify future restatement events.
