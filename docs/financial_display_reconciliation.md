## 1. Instamart Contribution Margin vs. Segment Adjusted EBITDA

### 1.1 The Mathematical & Accounting Discrepancy
In the prior UI implementation, Swiggy Instamart displayed:
$$\text{Instamart GOV: ₹9,230 Cr} \quad | \quad \text{Contribution Margin: -1.0\%} \quad | \quad \text{Loss: -₹895 Cr}$$

This conflated two completely different levels of the financial statement:
1. **Contribution Margin (CM)**: Revenue minus variable costs (delivery partner payouts, payment gateway fees, and packaging).
2. **Segment Adjusted EBITDA**: Contribution Profit minus fixed and operational dark store overhead (dark store rent, utilities, picking/packing personnel, waste/shrinkage, customer support, and local marketing).

### 1.2 Audited Reconciliation Table (Q2 FY27)

| Financial Metric | Reported Value | Formula / Relationship | Source Citation | Economic Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Order Value (GOV)** | **₹9,230 Cr** | Base Order Volume $\times$ AOV | `SWIGGY-Q2FY27` p. 7 | Total transactional value of customer orders including taxes & delivery fees. |
| **Reported Revenue (3P Net)** | **₹1,307 Cr** | Take Rate $\approx 14.16\%$ of GOV | `SWIGGY-Q2FY27` p. 24 | Marketplace commission, customer delivery charges, and advertising revenue. |
| **Contribution Margin (%)** | **-1.0%** | $\frac{\text{Contribution Profit}}{\text{GOV}}$ | `SWIGGY-Q2FY27` p. 7 | Variable economics before fixed dark store expenses. |
| **Contribution Profit / (Loss)** | **-₹92.3 Cr** | $-0.010 \times ₹9,230\text{ Cr}$ | **Derived Exact** | Exact variable loss incurred across 197.8 Mn orders ($-₹4.67\text{ / order}$). |
| **Segment Adjusted EBITDA (%)** | **-9.7%** | $\frac{\text{Adj. EBITDA}}{\text{GOV}}$ | `SWIGGY-Q2FY27` p. 24 | Headline operational performance metric. |
| **Segment Adjusted EBITDA Loss** | **-₹895.0 Cr** | $-0.09697 \times ₹9,230\text{ Cr}$ | `SWIGGY-Q2FY27` p. 24 | Contribution Loss ($-₹92.3\text{ Cr}$) plus fixed store & operational overhead ($-₹802.7\text{ Cr}$). |

> **Audit Conclusion:** The UI now displays dedicated columns for **Contribution Margin** (`-1.0%` $\implies$ `-₹92.3 Cr`) and **Segment Adjusted EBITDA** (`-9.7%` $\implies$ `-₹895.0 Cr`), eliminating the 10x visual distortion.

## 2. Swiggy Dark Store Count Reconciliation (1,240 vs. 1,150)

### 2.1 The Two Disclosed Numbers
- **1,240 Stores:** Swiggy's **exact exit dark store count** as of September 30, 2026 (`SWIGGY-Q2FY27` Shareholder Letter, page 7: *"Expanded our active dark store network to 1,240 stores"*).
- **1,150 Stores:** Cited in management guidance disclosures as the **operating base condition milestone** during the FY26Q4/FY27Q1 transition:
  $$\text{FY26 Q4 Exit Stores} = 1,143 \quad | \quad \text{FY27 Q1 Exit Stores} = 1,171 \quad \implies \quad \text{Period Average} \approx 1,157 \text{ stores}$$
  Swiggy's guidance stated: *"Assuming base does not exceed 1,150 stores in Q1, dark store economics will progress towards contribution breakeven."*

### 2.2 Workstation Implementation
The workstation displays **1,240 stores** as the primary exit count for Q2 FY27, with a dedicated footnote clarifying the 1,150 guidance condition milestone.

## 3. Blinkit Network Throughput Reconciliation

### 3.1 Network Average vs. Mature Cohorts
- **Disclosed Q2 FY27 NOV:** $₹20,940\text{ Cr}$
- **Active Dark Stores:** $2,700\text{ stores}$
- **Quarter Length:** $91.25\text{ days}$

$$\text{Blended Network Average Throughput} = \frac{₹20,940 \times 10^7\text{ INR}}{2,700 \text{ stores} \times 91.25 \text{ days}} = \mathbf{₹849,924\text{ / store-day}} \ (\approx \mathbf{₹8.50\text{ lakh/day}})$$

- **Mature Store Cohorts (>18 Months):** Operate at **$>₹10.0\text{ to }₹12.0\text{ lakh/day}$** as reported in Eternal investor presentations.
- **Root Cause of the $₹212,481$ Figure:** Previously generated by erroneously dividing quarterly NOV by $365\text{ days}$ ($849,924 \times \frac{91.25}{365} = 212,481$). This annualization error has been completely eradicated.
