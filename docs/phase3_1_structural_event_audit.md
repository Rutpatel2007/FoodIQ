# FoodIQ Phase 3.1: Independent Structural Break & Corporate Event Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

This document presents the accounting, legal, and operational audit of the 4 major corporate structural breaks cataloged in the **FoodIQ Structural Break Register**. 

Structural events represent material changes in accounting consolidation, commercial business models, or inorganic M&A scope that break historical time-series continuity.

---

## 2. Audit of Cataloged Corporate Events

### Event 1: SB-001 — Blinkit Transition to First-Party (1P) Owned Inventory Model
- **Company & Segment:** Eternal Limited / Quick Commerce (Blinkit)
- **Effective Quarter:** Q2 FY26 (August 2025 onwards)
- **Primary Citation:** Eternal Q2 FY26 Shareholder Letter, Page 12
- **Accounting Framework:** Ind AS 115 / IFRS 15 (Principal vs. Agent Considerations)
- **Operational Transformation:**
  - *Pre-Event:* 3P Marketplace Agency Model. Blinkit acted as an intermediary connecting merchants to consumers. Revenue was recognized as net commissions, delivery charges, and advertising.
  - *Post-Event:* 1P Principal Inventory Model. Blinkit purchases, owns, and holds retail inventory in dark stores. Under Ind AS 115, it acts as a principal, recording full gross merchandise sales as Adjusted Revenue, and inventory purchase costs in Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
- **Financial Impact Quantified:**
  - Adjusted Revenue jumped **+128.4% QoQ** (from ₹1,061 Cr in Q1 FY26 to ₹2,423 Cr in Q2 FY26).
  - Customer transacted Net Order Value ($NOV$) grew **+24.2% QoQ** (from ₹9,400 Cr to ₹11,679 Cr).
  - Over **80%** of the sequential revenue leap was an accounting artifact of gross billing.
  - Hyperpure external B2B revenue dropped by **55%** as ingredient transfers to Blinkit became internal intercompany eliminations.
- **LFL Feasibility:** **STRICTLY BLOCKED**. Eternal does not disclose product-level merchant wholesale acquisition costs (COGS). Fabricating an arbitrary 3P commission rate is rejected under financial integrity standards.
- **Audit Verdict:** **VERIFIED_ACCOUNTING_BREAK**.

### Event 2: SB-002 — Eternal Acquisition of Paytm Ticketing Business & District Launch
- **Company & Segment:** Eternal Limited / Going-Out (District)
- **Effective Quarter:** Q2 FY25 (Transaction closed August 27, 2024; partial stub in Q2 FY25, full quarter in Q3 FY25)
- **Primary Citation:** Eternal Q2 FY25 Shareholder Letter, Page 8
- **Accounting Framework:** Ind AS 103 (Business Combinations)
- **Operational Transformation:**
  - *Pre-Event:* Organic dining-out discovery, table reservations, and dining payment vouchers (Zomato Pay).
  - *Post-Event:* Consolidated live event ticketing, movie ticketing, and live concert experiences following the ₹2,048 Cr acquisition of Paytm's entertainment ticketing business, rebranded as District.
- **Financial Impact Quantified:**
  - Reported headline YoY GOV growth: **171.0%**
  - Disclosed organic like-for-like YoY GOV growth: **53.0%**
  - Acquired inorganic contribution: **118.0 percentage points**
- **LFL Feasibility:** **FEASIBLE**. Eternal explicitly disclosed the 53.0% organic benchmark in its Q2 FY25 Shareholder Letter, allowing clean isolation of organic vs inorganic performance.
- **Audit Verdict:** **VERIFIED_AUDITABLE**.

### Event 3: SB-003 — Swiggy Divestment of Lynks & Supply Chain Deconsolidation
- **Company & Segment:** Swiggy Limited / Supply Chain
- **Effective Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (Deconsolidated from Q3 FY27 onwards)
- **Primary Citation:** Swiggy Q2 FY27 Shareholder Letter, Page 15
- **Accounting Framework:** Ind AS 28 / Ind AS 110 (Loss of Control & Deconsolidation)
- **Operational Transformation:**
  - *Pre-Event:* Lynks third-party trucking and intracity logistics operations were fully consolidated in Swiggy's Supply Chain segment.
  - *Post-Event:* Swiggy divested its controlling stake. Lynks ceased to be consolidated from Q3 FY27 onwards, leaving Supply Chain focused on core restaurant partner grocery procurement.
- **Financial Impact Quantified:**
  - Segment Adjusted EBITDA recurring benefit: **~₹20 Cr per quarter** from eliminating Lynks operating losses.
  - Segment quarterly revenue reduction: **~₹200 Cr per quarter**.
- **LFL Feasibility:** **FEASIBLE**. Segment models can adjust historical revenue down by ₹200 Cr/quarter and add back ₹20 Cr/quarter EBITDA to evaluate core restaurant procurement efficiency.
- **Audit Verdict:** **VERIFIED_AUDITABLE**.

---

### Event 4: SB-004 — Swiggy Dineout Segment Formal Carve-Out
- **Company & Segment:** Swiggy Limited / Out-of-Home
- **Effective Quarter:** Q1 FY26 (June 2025 onwards)
- **Primary Citation:** Swiggy Q1 FY26 Shareholder Letter, Page 9; IPO Prospectus
- **Accounting Framework:** Ind AS 108 (Operating Segments)
- **Operational Transformation:**
  - *Pre-Event:* Dineout operations were partially reported under platform services without standalone quarterly run-rates.
  - *Post-Event:* Out-of-Home established as a distinct core reporting segment covering restaurant dining vouchers, table bookings, and SteppinOut live event experiences (targeting ₹10,000 Cr annualised GOV by FY29).
- **Financial Impact Quantified:**
  - Established baseline quarterly segment GOV of **~₹650 Cr**.
- **LFL Feasibility:** **FEASIBLE** with caveats regarding historical pre-FY25 quarterly restatements.
- **Audit Verdict:** **VERIFIED_AUDITABLE**.
