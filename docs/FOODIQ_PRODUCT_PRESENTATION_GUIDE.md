## 3. Comprehensive Product & Financial Glossary

- **Gross Order Value (GOV):** Total consumer order value including item prices, customer delivery fees, packaging charges, and taxes, net of restaurant-funded discounts.
- **Net Order Value (NOV):** Gross Order Value minus indirect customer taxes and platform-funded discounts. Blinkit's primary top-line metric.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Mean monetary value of an individual transaction ($\text{GOV or NOV} / \text{Order Count}$).
- **Take Rate:** Proportion of order flow recognized by the platform as revenue ($\text{Reported Revenue} / \text{Order Flow Denominator}$).
- **Contribution Margin (CM):** Order revenue minus direct variable fulfillment costs (delivery fleet fees, payment processing, packaging), expressed as % of GOV/NOV.
- **Segment Adjusted EBITDA:** Contribution Profit minus dark store rent, store electricity/utilities, picking staff wages, local marketing, and allocated segment overhead.
- **Ind AS 115 Principal vs. Agent Shift:** Indian accounting standard shift executed by Blinkit in Q2 FY26, switching from agent commission to owned inventory principal.
- **Chow Structural Break Test:** Econometric F-test evaluating whether coefficients in two time periods are significantly different, confirming regime shifts.
- **Benjamini-Hochberg FDR:** Statistical procedure controlling the False Discovery Rate among multiple hypothesis tests at a predefined threshold ($\alpha = 0.05$).
- **First-Difference Detrending:** Mathematical subtraction ($Y_t - Y_{t-1}$) to transform non-stationary time series into stationary differences, eliminating spurious correlation.
- **Bounded Monte Carlo Simulation:** Repeated random sampling of operational variables within empirical historical variance limits to forecast probability distributions.
- **Four-Tier Comparability Framework:** Algorithmic governance model classifying metrics into Level 1 (Direct), Level 2 (Adjusted), Level 3 (Directional), and Level 4 (Incomparable).
